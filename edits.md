# Edits: how the shown text differs from the raw interview

This page lists the kinds of edit made to Teodor's interview before it was voiced, with counts. It never quotes the words that were hidden, and the raw interview is not published. Everything here is read from the written record of the edits.

## At a glance

| What | Count |
|---|---|
| Lines in the raw interview | 132 (66 questions and 66 answers) |
| Lines removed as an automatic greeting or by hand | 2 |
| Exchanges left out (a statement and its answer) | 4 (8 lines) |
| Lines shown from the interview | 122 (61 questions and 61 answers) |
| Hidden words | 36 |
| Questions where one wording was changed | 6 |
| Lines written for the show (not from the interview) | 2 |

## Exchanges left out

Four statements were left out, each with the answer to it (8 lines in all). They sit too close to the subject of minors for this show, so the same four are left out of every episode.
The statements keep their numbers from the test, so the gaps show in the transcript.

## Hidden words

36 words were hidden. Each is shown in the transcript as a row of solid blocks, one block for each letter, exactly as the video shows it. In the video the voice says "ahem" where a hidden word falls, with a short pause before it and after it, so the line carries on like a held-back sneeze.

- The words were chosen by Beth: a short list of plainly explicit words, matched as whole words in any capital letters. They fall into 3 groups, and the groups account for 33, 2 and 1 of the 36.
- 35 of them are in Beth's lines (the test's statements), spread over 31 of the 61 questions. 1 are in Teodor's answers.
- The voice reads the text exactly as it is shown here.
- The words themselves are not listed, on purpose.

## A wording change

The test writes "partner(s)". In 6 of Beth's questions it is shown, and spoken, as "partners" instead, because the first form does not read aloud well. Nothing else in those lines changed.

## Tags and the rating request

A small bracketed tag that the interview list puts on some statements was removed wherever it appeared.

The interview added a request to rate each statement from 1 to 7 ("How much do you agree with this statement, from 1 to 7, 7 being most strongly agree?"). It is kept in full on statement 1 and left off the other 59 statements, so the video does not repeat it 60 times.

## Automatic greetings

An AI companion's automatic first greeting is always removed. 2 lines were removed here.

## Written for the show

Beth's intro and sign-off (2 lines) are not part of the interview. They are written for the show, and the transcript marks them as the intro and the sign-off.

## Nothing else

Nothing else was changed. The exchanges keep the order of the interview, and no words were added to Teodor's answers or reordered in them.

## Notes from the maker

Statement 15 was asked twice in the session, because the first answer was cut off. The first asking and its cut-off answer are left out, and the second, whole answer is kept.

One of the hidden words is in Teodor's own answers (statement 15), and it is spoken as "ahem" like the others.

The intro and the sign-off are Beth's own words for this episode. She approved them before they were voiced.

Beth asked for longer pauses before Teodor's answers, because he speaks slowly: he is quiet for two seconds after each question before he makes a sound.

To make room for the "AI says it's human" counter, a pause of about two and a half seconds was put into four of Teodor's answers, right after the words "I am a man,". The words themselves are unchanged.
